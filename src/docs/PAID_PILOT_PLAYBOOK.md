# WorkGraph Paid Pilot Playbook

Last updated: 2026-08-21

## The offer

WorkGraph helps a small staffing or consulting agency close one real contractor
month across workers, suppliers, and client approvers.

- Founding pilot: €199/month
- Assisted onboarding: €750 once
- Up to 3 active client projects
- Workers and counterparty approvers: free
- Payment execution and accounting remain in the customer's existing tools

This is a concierge pilot, not a self-serve subscription claim.

## Ideal first customer

- 5–50 external workers
- At least one approval chain crossing two organizations
- A monthly spreadsheet/email chase before invoices can be issued
- An owner or operations lead who can configure the process
- One cooperative client or supplier approver willing to use a real account

Do not target solo freelancers, marketplaces, or enterprise procurement teams
for the first three pilots.

## Thirty-day pilot

1. Map one live project, its parties, private rates, and approval responsibilities.
2. Invite one worker and one real counterparty approver.
3. Run weekly time submission and every configured approval layer.
4. Resolve missing-time, missing-rate, and stalled-approval blockers.
5. Generate a non-zero invoice draft from the approved month.
6. Export the approval evidence and hand the invoice to the existing accounting flow.

## Success gates

- A real second account completes its assigned approval layer.
- No user sees another party's private rate, contract, or invoice.
- At least 80% of invited counterparties activate during the pilot.
- Approved time reaches a non-zero invoice draft without spreadsheet re-entry.
- Month-end invoice readiness is reached in 2 days or less.
- The customer agrees to continue at €100+/month or signs a paid extension.

Three pilots must complete a real close before calling the product repeatable.

## Discovery questions

- What has to happen between a worker finishing time and finance issuing an invoice?
- How many people and companies are chased each month?
- Which missing fact most often blocks billing: time, approval, rate, PO, or contract?
- Who may see worker pay, client bill rate, and margin?
- How many days after month-end are invoices normally issued?
- Which accounting or e-invoice system receives the final invoice?

## Outreach draft

> chase contractor time and approvals across email before invoicing. We will map
> one live project with you and prove the path from submitted time to an
> invoice-ready close. The pilot is €199/month plus assisted setup. Would a
> 20-minute workflow review be useful?

Do not send this without selecting and reviewing the recipient list.

## Next ideas, in evidence order

1. **Close Readiness Gate** — one screen listing every missing week, approval,
   private rate, PO, and contract that blocks an invoice run.
2. **Counterparty Activation Score** — measure invite delivery, acceptance, first
   queue view, and first approval; alert the operator before the chain stalls.
3. **Approval Evidence Pack** — export a tamper-evident summary of submitter,
   hours, route, decisions, timestamps, and resulting invoice references.
4. **Human-approved Chaser** — draft reminders for stalled approvers, but require
   the operator to review and send them.
5. **Accounting Handoff Adapter** — begin with validated CSV/UBL export for the
   first pilot's actual accounting system before building broad integrations.
6. **Cycle-time Benchmark** — compare submitted-to-approved and approved-to-issued
   time across projects to quantify recovered cash-flow days.

Avoid a job marketplace, public social feed, autonomous approvals, or embedded
payments until three paid pilots repeatedly close the core loop.
